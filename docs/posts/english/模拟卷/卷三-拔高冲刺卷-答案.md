## 第五部分：写作（15 分）

### 参考范文（135 词）

```
Online Learning: A Double-Edged Sword

In recent years, online learning has gained tremendous popularity. Like any other innovation, it has both advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, online learning offers flexibility and convenience. Students can learn at their own pace and from anywhere, which is especially beneficial for those with busy schedules. It also provides access to a wider range of courses and resources that may not be available locally.

On the other hand, online learning lacks the face-to-face interaction that is essential for effective communication and collaboration. Some students may feel isolated and struggle to stay motivated without the structure of a traditional classroom.

In my opinion, online learning is a valuable tool but not a complete replacement for traditional education. The ideal approach is a blended learning model that combines the best of both worlds. Schools should invest in interactive platforms that facilitate real-time discussion and group work, while also providing opportunities for in-person learning when necessary.

In conclusion, online learning is here to stay. The key is to use it wisely and in combination with traditional methods.
```
