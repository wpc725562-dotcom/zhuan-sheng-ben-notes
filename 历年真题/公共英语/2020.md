## 第一部分：词汇与语法结构（共30小题，每小题1分，满分30分）

**Directions:** There are 30 incomplete sentences in this part. For each sentence there are four choices marked A, B, C and D. Choose the ONE answer that best completes the sentence.

**1. We study five days _____ week, and on Sundays we often play _____ football.**
- A. a; the
- B. the; a
- C. a; /
- D. the; /

**2. He told me that he _____ here for five minutes.**
- A. has come
- B. had arrived
- C. had been
- D. came

**3. — _____ are the Olympic Games held? — Every four years.**
- A. How soon
- B. How often
- C. How long
- D. When

**4. In today's class, he seemed _____ active than before.**
- A. much
- B. more
- C. most
- D. much more

**5. When the clock _____ twelve tonight, the deadline will have passed.**
- A. beats
- B. hits
- C. knocks
- D. strikes

**6. _____ receiving the book, she read the most interesting chapter first.**
- A. On
- B. At
- C. In
- D. For

**7. In the corner of the library, I found a book _____ cover was covered with ink.**
- A. who
- B. whose
- C. which
- D. that

**8. Our campus is _____ big _____ we need a bike.**
- A. such; that
- B. so; that
- C. too; to
- D. enough; to

**9. Judy hardly takes part in any class activities, _____?**
- A. does she
- B. doesn't she
- C. is she
- D. isn't she

**10. The students _____ outdoors when the visitors arrived.**
- A. were playing
- B. played
- C. have played
- D. had played

**11. I may fail, but I will go on _____.**
- A. yet
- B. anyhow
- C. whatever
- D. anyway

**12. _____ that everything goes well, we should take every detail into account.**
- A. To ensure
- B. Ensuring
- C. Ensured
- D. Having ensured

**13. Please give me one more day, and I will get _____ ready.**
- A. something
- B. anything
- C. everything
- D. nothing

**14. She was made _____ six days a week, from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m.**
- A. work
- B. working
- C. to work
- D. worked

**15. There are so many cars here that we don't know _____ to park.**
- A. what
- B. where
- C. wherever
- D. which

**16. The doctor warned that the bad weather would _____ more flu cases.**
- A. result from
- B. bring in
- C. result in
- D. bring on

**17. Online shopping, _____ properly, can save a lot of time, money and energy.**
- A. handled
- B. handling
- C. to handle
- D. handles

**18. Illegal hunting and trading of wild animals will be severely _____ in China.**
- A. punish
- B. punishing
- C. punished
- D. have punished

**19. The country will make double _____ to reduce the economic loss caused by the earthquake.**
- A. affect
- B. effect
- C. afford
- D. effort

**20. Many graduates choose to work in small cities _____ the high cost of living in big cities.**
- A. regardless of
- B. apart from
- C. as for
- D. due to

**21. He was busy writing a report, so he couldn't _____ the noise and had to close all the windows.**
- A. put up with
- B. come up with
- C. catch up with
- D. keep up with

**22. It is an online platform _____ people can buy and sell many kinds of items.**
- A. which
- B. where
- C. that
- D. what

**23. I like a house with a beautiful garden in front of _____, but I don't have enough money to buy _____.**
- A. one; one
- B. it; it
- C. it; one
- D. one; it

**24. A survey _____ that the number of obese people is increasing.**
- A. reflects
- B. releases
- C. remarks
- D. reveals

**25. He lifted the rock only _____ it drop on his own feet.**
- A. having
- B. to have
- C. had
- D. has

**26. With the development of economy, people's living standards have been _____ improved.**
- A. urgently
- B. significantly
- C. properly
- D. frequently

**27. It was in the museum _____ they came across a friend from high school.**
- A. which
- B. where
- C. that
- D. when

**28. In order to _____ information for the research project, she went to several libraries.**
- A. receive
- B. pick
- C. acquire
- D. gather

**29. — What shall we do tonight? — _____.**
- A. Go ahead
- B. Help yourself
- C. It's up to you
- D. No problem

**30. — Can I take a day off tomorrow, Mr. Smith? — I'm afraid not. I can't manage _____.**
- A. lonely
- B. alone
- C. along
- D. solely

| 题号 | 解析 |
|:---:|:---|
| 1 | **C**。a week表示"每周"；play football球类前不加冠词。 |
| 2 | **C**。过去完成时，arrive是瞬间动词不能与for five minutes连用。 |
| 3 | **B**。How often问频率，答语Every four years。 |
| 4 | **D**。than提示比较级，much修饰比较级more active。 |
| 5 | **D**。strike the clock敲钟。 |
| 6 | **A**。On/upon doing sth. 一……就……。 |
| 7 | **B**。whose在定语从句中作定语，修饰cover。 |
| 8 | **B**。so...that... 如此……以至于……。 |
| 9 | **A**。hardly否定，反意疑问句用肯定形式does she。 |
| 10 | **A**。when引导时间状语，主句用过去进行时。 |
