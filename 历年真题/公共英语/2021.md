## 第一部分：词汇与语法结构（共30小题，每小题1分，满分30分）

**Directions:** There are 30 incomplete sentences in this part. For each sentence there are four choices marked A, B, C and D. Choose the ONE answer that best completes the sentence.

**1. Bill, along with three other men, _____ to represent the union at tomorrow's meeting.**
- A. is
- B. was
- C. are
- D. were

**2. His wife bought the blue carpet, but he _____ bought the brown one.**
- A. would rather than
- B. rather had
- C. would rather have
- D. had better

**3. My wife and I _____ smoke, but we don't smoke any more.**
- A. use to
- B. used to
- C. were used to
- D. got used to

**4. They worked in a company _____ made computers.**
- A. which
- B. where
- C. how
- D. what

**5. The wind was _____ strong _____ we could no longer move forward.**
- A. such...that
- B. so...that
- C. too...to
- D. enough...to

**6. You cannot go to the party this afternoon _____ you behave yourself.**
- A. if
- B. whether
- C. because
- D. unless

**7. The sight _____ I saw in Shanghai left me a deep impression.**
- A. which
- B. what
- C. that
- D. where

**8. We suggested that he _____ Beijing next Tuesday.**
- A. left
- B. had left
- C. leave
- D. would leave

**9. _____ the child survived the accident is a miracle.**
- A. What
- B. Which
- C. That
- D. Whether

**10. The play _____ for five minutes. You are a little late.**
- A. has begun
- B. began
- C. had begun
- D. has been on

**11. You can count on John to look after your things while you are away.**
- A. count on
- B. count in
- C. count up
- D. count out

**12. Of the two teachers, the one in green is _____ patient.**
- A. more
- B. the more
- C. most
- D. the most

**13. The company's training plan is designed to help slow employees improve their _____.**
- A. efficiency
- B. fluency
- C. procession
- D. progress

**14. John _____ be in the classroom now. I saw him in the dining hall just now.**
- A. mustn't
- B. can't
- C. needn't
- D. shouldn't

**15. The citizens felt that the police had done _____ best in such a difficult situation.**
- A. its
- B. his
- C. their
- D. one's

**16. It was not until she got home _____ she remembered her appointment with the doctor.**
- A. that
- B. when
- C. where
- D. which

**17. The boy ran _____ his mother as soon as he saw her.**
- A. to
- B. into
- C. out
- D. away

**18. It was not long _____ people knew how to improve their living conditions.**
- A. after
- B. when
- C. until
- D. before

**19. Dogs are good friends because they can keep us _____ when we are lonely.**
- A. business
- B. company
- C. companion
- D. communication

**20. Don't be taken _____ by those products claiming to help you lose weight quickly.**
- A. in
- B. out
- C. off
- D. away

**21. — _____ does she look like? — She is tall and thin.**
- A. How
- B. What
- C. Which
- D. Who

**22. — Could you help me carry the bag? — _____**
- A. Yes, I could.
- B. With pleasure.
- C. It's my pleasure.
- D. That's all right.

**23. You should read this novel. It is highly _____ by our teacher.**
- A. suggested
- B. recommended
- C. commanded
- D. demanded

**24. — Of the three pairs of shoes, which one would you like to wear? — I'd like to take the black _____. I think it's the most durable.**
- A. one
- B. ones
- C. it
- D. that

**25. _____ by the teacher's words, she turned to one of her classmates for help.**
- A. Puzzling
- B. Puzzled
- C. Being puzzled
- D. Having puzzled

**26. — _____ are the Olympic Games held? — Every four years.**
- A. How long
- B. How soon
- C. How often
- D. How far

**27. _____ wants to offer old books to people in need is welcome to join us.**
- A. Who
- B. Whoever
- C. Anyone
- D. No matter who

**28. We called _____ you last night, but you were not at home.**
- A. on
- B. at
- C. for
- D. off

**29. My mother is a very _____ person, and she only spends money when it is most needed.**
- A. economic
- B. economical
- C. economy
- D. economics

**30. The scientist _____ that spending too much time staring at the phone could do harm to us.**
- A. remarked
- B. remained
- C. required
- D. repeated

## 参考范文

> **Dear Tom,**
>
> I'm glad to hear from you. Here is some information about our school gym.
>
> The gym is next to the library and opens from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. every day. There are many kinds of equipment, and professional coaches can provide personal training.
>
> If you want to use the gym, you need to make a reservation online or at the front desk. The monthly fee is about 100 yuan for students.
>
> I hope you will enjoy working out there. Looking forward to seeing you!
>
> **Best wishes,**
> **Li Hua**

## 解析速查

> 完整解析见下方及本地PDF。

| 题号 | 解析 |
|:---:|:---|
| 1 | **A**。主谓一致，谓语与 Bill 一致，用 is。 |
| 2 | **C**。would rather have done 表示宁愿做了某事。 |
| 3 | **B**。used to do sth. 过去常常做某事。 |
| 4 | **A**。定语从句缺主语，which 指代 company。 |
| 5 | **B**。so...that... 如此……以至于……。 |
| 6 | **D**。unless 除非，相当于 if not。 |
| 7 | **B**。what 引导主语从句，意为"所……的"。 |
| 8 | **C**。suggest 后接虚拟语气，用动词原形 leave。 |
| 9 | **C**。that 引导主语从句，从句完整。 |
| 10 | **D**。has been on 表状态，可与时间段连用。 |
| 11 | **A**。count on 依赖，指望。 |
| 12 | **A**。两者比较用比较级 more patient。 |
| 13 | **A**。efficiency 效率。 |
| 14 | **B**。can't 表示否定推测，不可能。 |
| 15 | **C**。police 为复数，用 their。 |
| 16 | **A**。it was not until...that... 强调句型。 |
| 17 | **A**。ran to 跑向。 |
| 18 | **D**。It was not long before... 没过多久就……。 |
| 19 | **B**。keep sb. company 陪伴某人。 |
| 20 | **A**。be taken in 被欺骗，被吸引。 |
