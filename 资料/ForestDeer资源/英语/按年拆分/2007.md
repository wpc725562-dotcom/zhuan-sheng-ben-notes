                                                                                                        June 7,2007
  Dear Wang Hong,
        I'm writing to ask you to excuse me for not being polite to Gao Yan in your birthday party.
  I do know that I should not have quarreled with Gao Yan when he blamed me for being late for the
  party.
        I was hurrying to the party when I met with a heavy traffic jam on the way to your birthday
  party. As a result, I was in a very bad mood for being thirty minutes late. I lost control of myself
  when Gao Yan blamed me. I ruined the happy atmosphere of your birthday party. I now apologize to
  you for what I did at your home. I will draw lessons from it and make sure to keep such things
  from happening again.
        I do hope that you can forgive me for my rudeness.
