  76.
                                                                                                     March 15, 2006
  Dear Sirs,
        I hope to draw your attention to the reduction in the standard of food provided by the school's
  canteen.
        I really appreciate the well-prepared food which used to be available in the canteen. However,
  in the early of March the number of dishes was dramatically reduced, and there was a obvious
  deterioration in quality. I have tried the canteen periodically since then and have always been
  served overcooked, or undercooked food. The sharp decline in the number of students dining in the
  canteen makes me point out the problem by writing this letter.
        I would be very grateful if you could explain to me why decline in standard has occurred, and
  whether the school intends to take steps to rectify the situation.
                                                                                                 Yours sincerely,
                                                                                                          Zhang Ning
